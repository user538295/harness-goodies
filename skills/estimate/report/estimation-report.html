Metric explorer

Each dot is one feature (hover for its name). Boxes show the interquartile range per SP value, the thin line runs from min to max, the bar marks the median, and the dashed line is the power-law fit metric ≈ c·SPb. The y axis is logarithmic. Hollow amber diamonds are features that are only estimated: they sit at their estimated SP on the plan-based tabs (Tasks, Estimated hours, Phases) and take no part in any statistic or fit.

How the metrics grow as SP grows

Median of each metric per SP value, divided by its median at SP . The dashed line is perfect proportionality (SP ÷ ). Lines above it grow faster than SP, lines below it grow slower. Both axes are logarithmic.
